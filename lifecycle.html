From Forest Harvest to Landfill Fate

The Life-Cycle of a Single Sheet of Paper

Paper seems organic and harmless on our desks, but its cradle-to-grave trajectory consumes tremendous freshwater, harsh bleaching chemicals, thermal energy, and freight corridors. Explore all 7 industrial phases below.

Phase 1 of 7

Forest Harvesting & Timber Clearing

Mature trees (such as eucalyptus, pine, and birch) are felled. Over 40% of all industrial timber harvested globally enters the paper manufacturing supply chain, contributing to local soil erosion and disruption of native wildlife canopies.

Where Does Paper Pile Up in Daily Life?
