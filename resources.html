Scientific Integrity

Sources, Bibliography & References

Every statistic, life-cycle metric, and resource efficiency figure presented in this research project is grounded in peer-reviewed reports from recognized international environmental agencies, including the FAO, WWF, US EPA, and IPCC.

[1] Global Production Baseline

FAO Forest Products Yearbook & Production Statistics

Publisher: Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO)
Referenced Data: Global annual paper and paperboard production exceeding 420 million metric tons; historical production trajectories from 1990 to 2025.

View Source Repository →
[2] Industrial Timber Harvest

WWF Pulp and Paper Global Environmental Status Report

Publisher: World Wildlife Fund (WWF International)
Referenced Data: 40% of all commercially logged industrial wood enters the global pulp and paper supply chain; plantation vs natural old-growth forest dynamics.

View WWF Industry Profile →
[3] Landfill Volume & Decomposition

Advancing Sustainable Materials Management: Fact Sheet

Publisher: United States Environmental Protection Agency (US EPA)
Referenced Data: Paper and paperboard accounting for 26% of municipal solid waste (MSW) by weight; anaerobic breakdown kinetics generating methane gas.

Access EPA Waste Registry →
[4] Freshwater Footprint Methodology

Environmental Paper Network: Paper Calculator Methodology

Publisher: Environmental Paper Network (EPN International)
Referenced Data: 5–10 liters freshwater consumed per standard virgin sheet of A4 paper during wood chipping, chemical cooking, washing, and steam generation.

View EPN Paper Methodology →
[5] Digital vs Physical Life-Cycle Carbon

IPCC Working Group III: Mitigation of Climate Change

Publisher: Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) & Our World in Data
Referenced Data: Lifecycle comparative assessment: 11.2 kg CO₂e per 1,000 virgin sheets vs 0.8 kg CO₂e for equivalent cloud document access under 75% renewable grid mix.
