Privacy Policy & Data Ethics

How user information, pledges, and local device storage are treated across this awareness platform.

1. Zero Third-Party Tracking

This website is completely free of third-party advertising trackers, profiling pixels, commercial data brokers, and biometric scripts. We do not sell, rent, or monetize visitor data under any circumstances.

2. Browser LocalStorage Usage

To maintain responsive speed and work without server-side database requirements, certain user interactions are saved locally within your own browser via HTML5 localStorage:

  • Theme Preference: Saves whether you selected dark mode or light mode (theme).
  • Application Preferences: Saves interactive state and preference configurations.
  • Pledge & Messages: Keeps a local confirmation copy of your pledge email and submitted feedback.

This data remains strictly stored on your own device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser cache.

3. Citations & Fair Use

All statistical indicators from the FAO, WWF, and US EPA are utilized strictly under fair use for non-commercial environmental educational purposes.

4. Contact for Inquiries

For questions regarding data ethics or organizational sustainability guidelines, please reach out via our Contact Page.
